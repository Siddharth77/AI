# Sample Questions

## RAG only

- What are the must-visit attractions in Singapore?
- Which neighbourhoods are best for culture and heritage?
- How should a tourist travel around Singapore using public transport?
- Suggest indoor attractions in Singapore.

## MCP only

- What is the weather in Singapore for the next three days?
- Is rain expected during my trip next week?
- Convert INR 50000 to SGD.
- How much is 200 SGD in INR?

## Combined

- Create a three-day Singapore itinerary for next week and adjust it according to the weather forecast.
- I have a budget of INR 60000. Convert it to SGD and suggest a three-day itinerary.
- Suggest outdoor attractions and replace them with indoor options if rain is expected.
- Plan a family trip and include the latest weather forecast.

## Good follow-ups for context retention

- Make day 2 more family friendly.
- Reduce walking and use MRT options where possible.
- Keep the budget under the SGD amount you just calculated.
- Replace museums with food-focused stops.
